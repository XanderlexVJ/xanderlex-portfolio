Alexander
Lubich.

My journey into photography didn't start within studio walls, but in the quiet of a morning forest. For me, a camera is not just a tool for capturing reality; it's a way to slow down, hold my breath, and listen to nature speaking.

I find inspiration in the geometry of shadows on wet stones, the whisper of the wind through the canopy, and the fleeting rays of light piercing through heavy fog. I don't stage scenes or direct the frame. I am simply a patient observer in this vast, living mechanism.

Nature paints the most perfect pictures. My only task is to preserve its fragile, untouched poetry a second before the light changes.
